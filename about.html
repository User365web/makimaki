About makiconvertpro

makiconvertpro is a free tool that turns CSV files into Excel spreadsheets.

Most online converters upload your file to a server. makiconvertpro does the work inside your browser instead, so customer lists, invoices and exports stay on your device.

What it does

  • Reads CSV, TSV and plain-text table files
  • Detects the separator and text encoding
  • Creates a standard .xlsx workbook with a styled header row
  • Keeps leading zeros and long numbers safe

How it is built

The site is static HTML, CSS and JavaScript hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It uses no third-party scripts and no tracking cookies.
